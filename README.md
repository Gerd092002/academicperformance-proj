For All Users: Start by visiting the website and logging in with your username and password. 
Once logged in, you’ll see a dashboard made just for your role. When you're finished, 
always click Logout in the top corner to keep your account secure. 

If you are a teacher: Use your dashboard to create assignments, grade student work, and manage your classes. Click "My Subjects" to view your classes, then select a specific subject to view students and manage tasks. To grade, select an assignment to enter grades and feedback. To add new assignments, go to the subject's Assignments tab, click + Add Assignment, fill in the details, and publish. You can also manage student enrollments through the Enrolled Students or Pending Requests tabs. 

If you are a student: Your dashboard shows upcoming assignments and your current grades. Click on the subject you are enrolled in, then navigate to the Assignments tab. Find the task, upload your file, and click Submit Now. 

If You’re an Admin: You can add and remove users and subjects from the Teachers/Students Management and Subjects Management sections. To create a new account, go to the respective user tab, fill in the user’s details, and click Add New Teacher/Student. To add a subject, go to Subjects Management, enter its name, assign a teacher, and save.
